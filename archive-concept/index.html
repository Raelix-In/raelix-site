How I would build the whole archive

One connected library

Artworks, science, places and stories live in one searchable CMS and link to each other, so every cake can lead to a field site, a paper or another piece.

Every artwork is its own world

Each piece gets an interaction like this one, and selected projects open as ArcGIS Story Maps. Mobile gets its own design, not a shrunk desktop page.

Yours to run

You add projects and edit text yourself, with no developer needed. Accessible by default: keyboard, screen reader and reduced-motion support. I would build in Framer or a custom stack, whichever serves the site best.

Made in an afternoon as a way of saying hello. The real thing would use your artworks, your words and your science.
