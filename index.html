Common questions

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. After a short conversation we send a written scope and quote. If you have a fixed budget, tell us up front and we will scope the work to fit it.

Can you take over code that another team wrote?

Yes. We start by reviewing the code, hosting and access, then tell you honestly whether to fix it, upgrade it or rebuild it.

Who owns the code and the accounts?

You do. Domains, hosting, databases and code repositories are set up under your own accounts.

Do you look after the project after launch?

Yes. We offer monthly support for fixes, hosting, updates and new features, and you can end it whenever you like.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short description of what you want built or fixed. If you already have code, hosting or other accounts, mention them. A budget range helps us suggest the right approach.
